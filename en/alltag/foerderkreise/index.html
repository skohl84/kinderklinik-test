    Friends' associations and foundations

    Many offers for children and families would not exist without supporters: playrooms, work with parents, sports therapy and research. Thank you to everyone who supports us. Current donations and activities appear in the feed.

    Förderkreis Hilfe für krebskranke Kinder e.V. Aachen

    Supports the section of paediatric haematology, oncology and stem cell transplantation. Kullenhofwinkel 26, 52074 Aachen, Tel. +49 241 86131, krebskrankekinder.de

    Friends' association of the Social Paediatric Centre

    Supports the work of the SPZ. foerderverein-spz.de

    Stiftung Universitätsmedizin Aachen

    Funds projects at Uniklinik, for example KARLOTTA in paediatric gastroenterology. stiftung-universitaetsmedizin-aachen.de

    Bunter Kreis in der Region Aachen e.V.

    Aftercare for families, for example with type 1 diabetes. bunterkreis-aachen.de

    Ronald McDonald House

    A home away from home for families near the hospital. [link]
