    Your stay with us

    A hospital stay is exciting for children and parents. Here you will find what applies to all wards. Details are on the page of each ward.

    Admission

    For a planned stay you need a hospital referral from your paediatrician or GP, except in emergencies, and your child's insurance card. Please bring medical reports and X-ray, CT or MRI findings if the condition has been treated elsewhere.

    Please bring

    • Day and night clothes, toiletries, child's toothbrush
    • Familiar things: cuddly toy, comforter, favourite toy
    • Medication plan with names and doses
    • Special infant formula or tube feed that is not always in stock
    • For parents: comfortable clothes and toiletries

    We provide towels, bed linen, nappies and the usual care products.

    Parents staying overnight

    On many wards one parent can stay overnight in the child's room. A parent who is admitted with the child receives breakfast, lunch and dinner on the ward. For planned admissions of school-age children, please check with your health insurer beforehand.

    Breastfeeding mothers and families from further away can stay at the Ronald McDonald House, about 10 minutes' walk away. There is a free night-time shuttle to the hospital for breastfeeding mothers.

    Visiting

    • Parents are welcome on all wards at any time.
    • For other visitors, fixed hours or arrangements apply depending on the ward. The number and length of visits should suit the child's condition.
    • To protect against infection, children under 14 may only enter the wards after consultation, and some wards not at all. Siblings and friends can meet in the visitors' room outside the ward.
    • Rest periods are important for recovery.

    Phone and TV

    Most beds have a telephone and TV; the phone can be activated for a fee. Mobile phones may not be used on the neonatal unit and the intensive care unit because of the medical equipment. Public phones are available on the paediatric level and in the entrance area.
