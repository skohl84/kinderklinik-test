    Play, sport and therapy dog

    Playroom

    In the department's playroom, qualified child care workers look after the children. Many wards also have their own playrooms with games and books. In good weather there is a playground on the roof terrace, except for children with infectious diseases.

    Sports therapy in paediatric oncology

    Chemotherapy and long periods in bed cause tiredness and weakness and can affect coordination and mood. In sports therapy, children and adolescents receive exercise therapy tailored to them during treatment.

    • Toddlers: playful movement so that motor development continues, with flexibility, balance and strength
    • Children: training for endurance, strength and flexibility, playful and motivating, so that the joy of movement remains
    • Adolescents: sport that is fun and provides balance, with attention to physical changes and strain

    The aims are fewer side effects, more independence and better quality of life during and after treatment.

    Therapy dog Lino

    Lino accompanies children in paediatric oncology as a therapy dog and is part of the holistic treatment concept. [Short text and photo, link to previous news]
