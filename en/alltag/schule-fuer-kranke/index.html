    Hospital school

    Children from primary school age who stay longer or more often receive regular lessons. Teachers coordinate with the child's own school so that nobody falls behind. Lessons take place in the classroom or, in case of infectious disease, in the patient room. In the KIPS day clinic, school is a fixed part of the daily routine.
