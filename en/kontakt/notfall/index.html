    Emergency

    Since 15 December 2025, all paediatric emergency patients register at any time on level −2 of the emergency department.

    In a life-threatening emergency, call the ambulance on 112.

    What to bring

    • Your child's insurance card and child health record
    • Medication plan, important medical reports
    • In case of poisoning: packaging or remains of the substance
