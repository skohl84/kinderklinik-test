    Make an appointment

    How it works

    1. Referral: your child is usually seen with a referral from your paediatrician or GP, who issues an outpatient clinic authorisation form.
    2. Registration: by phone or with the fax referral form. Briefly describe your child's symptoms so we can find the right clinic. Some specialties have their own routes, see Specialties.
    3. What to bring: referral, insurance card, child health record, medical reports, external imaging on CD, medication plan.
    4. Allow time: plan at least 60 minutes for the walk from the car park and registration.
