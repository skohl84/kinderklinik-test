    Online prescription requests

    Patients under our care can request repeat prescriptions for medicines we have prescribed.

    Information we need

    • Name of the medicine
    • Strength
    • Pack size
    • Quantity
    • Your name
    • Your email address for queries

    [Embed the existing online form here]

    Collection

    The prescription is ready at the SPZ after two working days. Before signing, a doctor carefully reviews every medicine, even if it has been taken for a long time.

    If the insurance card has not yet been read this quarter, prescriptions are only issued on presentation of the card and the referral.
