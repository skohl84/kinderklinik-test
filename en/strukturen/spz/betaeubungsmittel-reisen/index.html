    Controlled medicines when travelling

    Anyone travelling with medicines covered by the German Narcotics Act needs a certificate. There are two forms, depending on the destination.

    Forms

    • Travel within the Schengen area (PDF, German), please complete sections B and C
    • Travel to other countries (PDF, German), please complete sections C and D

    How it works

    1. Download and complete the right form.
    2. Send it back to the SPZ: [SPZ email address].
    3. We check and complete the certificate.
    4. The public health office at your place of residence approves it.

    The certificate is ready for collection at the SPZ after two working days.
