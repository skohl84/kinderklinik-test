    Ward Ki07, General paediatrics

    On Ki07 we care for infants, children and adolescents with general paediatric conditions and with gastroenterological and nephrological diseases. Many stays last only a few days, for example for minor procedures under anaesthesia. [Previously infant and toddler ward with about 1,000 children per year; check age group and figures for the new focus]

    Facilities

    • 27 beds in 12 double and 3 single rooms; single rooms are allocated for medical reasons
    • Ward playroom and the department's playroom with child care workers
    • Playground on the roof terrace in good weather, not for infectious conditions
    • Baby clothes, nappies, towels and bed linen are provided; infant and toddler food from the milk kitchen

    Parents and visitors

    Parents are welcome around the clock. One parent or another close carer can stay overnight in the room, with a fold-away bed, bathroom and meals at no extra cost. Other visitors please come during visiting hours, which nursing staff can tell you. To protect against infection, children under 14 may not enter the ward.

    Talking to the team

    Longer conversations are best held during the ward round. Otherwise, please arrange an appointment with the ward doctor.

    Please bring

    • Hospital referral (except in emergencies) and your child's insurance card
    • Medical reports, X-ray, CT or MRI findings if the condition was treated elsewhere
    • Familiar things: cuddly toy, comforter, toys, usual routines
    • Child's toothbrush, special food, e.g. for metabolic disorders
    • For parents: comfortable clothes and toiletries
