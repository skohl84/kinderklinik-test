    Ward Ki04, Neuropaediatrics and Social Paediatrics

    On Ki04 we treat school-age children and adolescents with neurological conditions. It is also the preferred general paediatrics ward for children with endocrine disorders and diabetes mellitus, for example at diagnosis and for training. We also care for children with cystic fibrosis, infections and other chronic conditions here. [please check whether cystic fibrosis and infections are still on this ward]

    Parents

    We welcome parents of acutely or chronically ill children. For planned admissions of school-age children, please check with your health insurer beforehand whether a parent can be admitted too. Parents can also stay at the Ronald McDonald House.

    Please bring

    • Day and night clothes, toiletries, favourite cuddly toy or game
    • Medical reports and X-rays, ideally on CD
    • For regular medication: current plan with names and doses

    School and play

    For longer or frequent stays there are regular lessons, coordinated with the child's school, in the classroom or, in case of infection, in the patient room. Two child care workers run the department's playroom, and the ward has games and books.
