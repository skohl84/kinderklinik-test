    Ward Ki08, Paediatric intensive care unit

    The paediatric intensive care unit has 18 beds for modern intensive care. We treat children and adolescents of all ages. A particular focus is the care of critically ill newborns and very preterm infants by the neonatology section. Older children come to us with severe illness, after accidents or after surgery.

    Team

    Consultants: PD Dr. med. Konrad Heimann, PD Dr. med. Mark Schoberer, Dr. med. Sonja Trepels-Kottek, Dr. med. Elmar Rose, Dr. med. Sarah Essers. Nurse manager: Julia Müller.

    Parents

    Your presence is welcome at any time and important for recovery. Preterm and newborn infants may cuddle with you early on (kangaroo care). You can stay at the Ronald McDonald House; breastfeeding mothers can use a free night-time shuttle to the hospital. Admitted parents receive three meals.

    To protect against infection, children under 14 may not enter the intensive care unit. Mobile phones interfere with the medical equipment and are not allowed.

    Sleep laboratory

    In the sleep laboratory we examine children with suspected night-time breathing disorders. You need a referral from your paediatrician; admission is at 8 pm. Please bring nightwear, toiletries and a familiar cuddly toy.

    There is a parents' evening for parents of children with a home monitor.
