    Section of Neonatology and Paediatric Intensive Care

    The section runs the neonatal unit of the perinatal centre at Uniklinik RWTH Aachen. Our aim is family-centred, developmentally supportive care for preterm and at-risk newborns that reflects the latest evidence.

    Parents are part of the team

    • Parents receive practical and theoretical training and are involved in nursing and physiotherapy during the stay.
    • Ongoing parent training is available as needed.
    • Newborns, parents and siblings should be together as often, as long and as early as possible.
    • Our staff are trained in family-centred care.

    Supporting development from day one

    All measures focus on development from the first day of life: medical procedures, developmentally appropriate stimulation and guidance for parents.

    Follow-up

    • Developmental support continues seamlessly after discharge.
    • All preterm infants are followed up at the Social Paediatric Centre at least until school entry, with standardised tests at fixed times.
    • At three months, infants are seen in the neonatal outpatient clinic.

    Teaching and research

    We train medical students, paediatricians, paediatric nurses and physiotherapists, and offer specialist training in paediatric intensive care nursing and in neonatology. Visiting doctors, medical students and school interns are welcome.

    Our research aims to ensure that preterm infants do not differ from term-born children after discharge. More under research groups.
