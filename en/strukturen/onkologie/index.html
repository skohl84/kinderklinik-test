    Section of Paediatric Haematology, Oncology and Stem Cell Transplantation

    We care for children and adolescents from birth to 18 years with malignant diseases, blood and clotting disorders, immune disorders and vascular malformations. Treatment takes place on the paediatric oncology ward Ki01, on the day ward and in the outpatient clinic.

    Treatment

    • Classic approaches: surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy
    • Modern approaches: high-dose therapy, immunotherapy, allogeneic stem cell transplantation, CAR T-cell therapy
    • Treatment according to national and international guidelines and GPOH recommendations; trial centre for clinical studies
    • Cooperation with all departments and institutes involved in diagnosis, treatment and follow-up
    • Interprofessional team of psychology, social work, child care and sports therapy, plus therapy dog Lino

    Networks

    • Centre for Integrated Oncology (CIO) Aachen Bonn Cologne Düsseldorf
    • European Reference Network for Paediatric Cancer (ERN PedCan)
    • Recognised paediatric oncology centre of the GPOH and the Federal Joint Committee
    • Support group for children with cancer, Aachen

    Research and teaching

    Our focus is translational research on nasopharyngeal carcinoma. The section hosts the international NPC-2016 registry and the phase 2 trial NPC-Nivo: NPC study group. We offer lectures, clinical electives, doctoral theses and specialist training in paediatric haematology and oncology.
