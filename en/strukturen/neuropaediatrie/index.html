    Section of Neuropaediatrics and Social Paediatrics

    Neuropaediatrics is neurology for children and adolescents. We treat disorders of the central nervous system, that is the brain and spinal cord, as well as disorders of nerves and muscles. Because these often require several specialties and all of them are available at Uniklinik, comprehensive care is possible in one place.

    Where we treat

    Emergency

    Paediatric emergencies register on level −2 of the emergency departmentMore →
    • Ward Ki04: severe acute conditions, planned investigations such as sleep EEG, long-term EEG or lumbar puncture, children needing close observation.
    • University outpatient clinic: assessment and long-term treatment of specific neurological conditions, for example epilepsy.
    • Social Paediatric Centre: children and adolescents with complex conditions and developmental disorders, multidisciplinary team. Head: Dr. med. Reinhild Damen.
    • Emergencies: children with acute neurological problems, day and night.

    Interdisciplinary centres

    • Neuromuscular Centre Aachen
    • Neurovascular Centre Aachen

    We work together with

    Neurology, orthopaedics, ENT and phoniatrics, ophthalmology, child and adolescent psychiatry, paediatric cardiology, human genetics and neuropathology at Uniklinik.
