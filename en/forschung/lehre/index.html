    Teaching

    The department is involved in all medical degree programmes: the old and new licensing regulations and the model degree programme. All courses and dates are listed in the RWTH CAMPUS system. Because several regulations run in parallel, dates may change during the semester, so please check regularly.

    Certificates

    Please check with the student office which regulation applies to you: old regulations, transitional rules, new regulations or model programme. The requirements for certificates are listed in the CAMPUS system.

    Clinical electives, block placement and final-year rotation

    Clinical electives are registered with the department's student office. Information on block placements and the final year: Studies and final year.

    Doctoral theses

    Ask the leads of the research groups and see Doctoral theses.
