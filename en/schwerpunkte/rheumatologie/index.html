    Paediatric Rheumatology

    We treat children and adolescents with rheumatic diseases so that they can take part in school and leisure activities again.

    Conditions

    • Juvenile idiopathic arthritis in its various forms
    • Systemic lupus erythematosus
    • Dermatomyositis

    Typical reasons for referral

    Joint complaints, unexplained episodes of fever, raised inflammatory markers or weakness.

    Diagnosis and treatment

    Laboratory tests, ultrasound and MRI of the joints, modern medicines and physiotherapy.

    Referral

    1. Referral from your paediatrician or GP
    2. Outpatient clinic authorisation form
    3. Appointment via the outpatient office

    Activity: Charity bike ride "U can ride for arthritis" · Research: Transcriptional regulation of the immune system
