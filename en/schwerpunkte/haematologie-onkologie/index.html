    Paediatric Haematology, Oncology and Haemostaseology

    We care for children and adolescents from birth to 18 years with leukaemia, solid tumours and brain tumours, diseases of the blood and blood clotting, immune system disorders and vascular malformations. Organisation and wards: Oncology section.
