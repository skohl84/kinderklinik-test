    Paediatric Nephrology and Paediatric Urology

    Paediatric nephrology and paediatric urology at Uniklinik RWTH Aachen care jointly for children and adolescents with diseases of the kidneys and urinary tract. This close link is a strength of our site: diagnosis, surgical planning and kidney care are closely coordinated. Particular areas of expertise are congenital anomalies of the kidney and urinary tract (CAKUT), lower urinary tract obstruction (LUTO) including follow-up after prenatal shunt therapy, hereditary and rare kidney diseases, and acute dialysis.

    PD Dr. med. Stefan KohlHead of paediatric nephrology · paediatric nephrology, transplantation medicine
    Prof. Dr. med. Dorothea RohrmannHead of the paediatric urology section, Department of Urology

    Making an appointment

    Children and adolescents are seen with a referral from their paediatrician or GP. To make an appointment, please contact us by email or fax.

    • Paediatric nephrology: clinic on Monday and Tuesday mornings. Urgent appointments are possible on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday afternoons after consultation.
    • Paediatric urology: clinic on Mondays from 9 am to 4 pm and by arrangement.
    • Please bring: referral, letters and findings, ultrasound images, current medication plan. For bladder dysfunction, also a fluid intake and voiding diary over [number] days: download template (PDF) [add file].
