    Surgical procedures

    [First draft, to be checked and completed by paediatric urology]

    When surgery is needed, we choose the gentlest possible approach. Where possible, we operate minimally invasively through small incisions or endoscopically through the urethra. After surgery, children are cared for on ward Ki03.

    • Pyeloplasty for ureteropelvic junction obstruction
    • Ureteral reimplantation for reflux or megaureter, endoscopic reflux treatment
    • Incision of posterior urethral valves and ureteroceles
    • Reconstructive surgery for neurogenic bladder, e.g. bladder augmentation
    • Continent urinary diversion
    • Removal of kidney stones
    • Hypospadias repair
    • Surgery for undescended testes
