    Paediatric urology clinic

    [First draft, to be checked and completed by paediatric urology]

    Paediatric urology is a section of the Department of Urology [check link] and works closely with paediatric nephrology.

    In the paediatric urology clinic we examine and advise children and adolescents with congenital and acquired conditions of the urinary tract and external genitalia. These include hydronephrosis, vesicoureteral reflux, posterior urethral valves, bladder dysfunction and incontinence, undescended testes, hypospadias and epispadias, and kidney stones.

    Together with you we discuss whether non-surgical treatment is sufficient or surgery makes sense, and when the best time for it is.

    Diagnostics

    • Ultrasound of the kidneys, urinary tract and genitalia, alternatively MRI
    • Uroflowmetry and post-void residual measurement
    • Contrast X-ray or ultrasound of the bladder (VCUG or contrast-enhanced voiding urosonography)
    • Bladder pressure measurement (urodynamics)
    • Renal scintigraphy
    • MR urography
