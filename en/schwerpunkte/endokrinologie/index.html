    Paediatric Endocrinology and Diabetology

    We care for children and adolescents with hormonal disorders and diabetes. Many of our patients have chronic conditions and need coordinated care across specialties and good education for themselves and their families.

    Our paediatric diabetes team is recognised as an "excellent clinic for kids with type 1 diabetes" by diabetes-kids.

    Endocrine clinic

    • Hormonal disorders of growth and puberty
    • Disorders of bone metabolism
    • Variations of sex development
    • Disorders of the pituitary, thyroid, adrenal glands, gonads and parathyroid glands
