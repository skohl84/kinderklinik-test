    DIA-KARLOTTA, learning about diabetes through play

    DIA-KARLOTTA is a learning quiz developed by our diabetes team led by Dr. med. Angeliki Pappa. Children and adolescents with type 1 diabetes can test their knowledge while waiting. The mascot is a little bird called KARLOTTA, short for "Kids + Adolescents Research Learning On Tablet Teaching Aachen".

    How it works

    • Answer diabetes questions and collect points
    • A jump-and-run game for skill and speed
    • A virtual treatment room for a treasure hunt

    Aims

    Better knowledge of the condition, more self-efficacy and better adherence. The DIA-KARLOTTA studies, and since 2025 DIA-KARLOTTA 2.0, run in Aachen and at four other sites. One focus is adolescents aged 16 and over and their transition to adult care.

    Awards

    • 2022: 2nd place and audience award in the bytes4diabetes competition
    • Leonard Thompson Memorial Award of the AGPD

    The concept is also available for inflammatory bowel disease: KARLOTTA in gastroenterology.
