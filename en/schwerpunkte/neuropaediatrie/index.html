    Neuropaediatric university outpatient clinic

    In our university outpatient clinic we assess specific neurological conditions and treat them long-term. Organisation, ward and SPZ: Neuropaediatrics and Social Paediatrics section.

    Areas of focus

    • General neuropaediatrics
    • Epilepsies
    • Neuromuscular diseases
    • Inflammatory diseases of the nervous system, e.g. multiple sclerosis
    • Cerebrovascular disease
    • Hydrocephalus
    • Neurometabolic diseases
    • Brain malformations
    • Headache
    Level 3

    Key areas

    From epilepsy to hydrocephalus, how we treatMore →

    EEG and electrophysiology laboratory

    Electroencephalography (EEG), visual and auditory evoked potentials (VEP, AEP), nerve conduction studies (NCS) and electromyography (EMG).

    How to get an appointment

    1. You need a referral from your paediatrician or GP.
    2. Call the outpatient clinic and describe your child's symptoms. A doctor checks whether we are the right clinic.
    3. Bring previous findings: medical reports, child health record, external imaging on CD.
    4. Allow at least 60 minutes for the walk from the car park and registration.
