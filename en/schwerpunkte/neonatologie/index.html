    Neonatology

    The neonatology section runs the neonatal unit of the Uniklinik perinatal centre: 36 beds on the neonatal ward and up to 18 intensive care beds. Organisation and team: Neonatology section.

    Everything under one roof

    Obstetrics and neonatology are under one roof. A resuscitation room in the delivery suite ensures that a paediatrician is immediately available in an emergency.

    What we treat

    Heart malformations, respiratory failure, asphyxia, infections, seizures and metabolic diseases.

    Equipment: modern ventilators with nitric oxide and high-frequency oscillation, incubators, monitoring, ultrasound and echocardiography, bronchoscopy, bedside laboratory, X-ray.

    We work closely with ophthalmology, paediatric audiology, orthopaedics, neurosurgery, abdominal and cardiac surgery.

    Warmth and closeness

    Alongside high-end medicine, we value a humane approach and involving parents early: kangaroo care even for the smallest babies, breastfeeding support, guidance in caring for the baby, accommodation at the Ronald McDonald House and a free night-time shuttle for breastfeeding mothers.

    Care at home

    Where needed, we involve community paediatric nursing, physiotherapy, early intervention and medical follow-up.

    Parents' evening, home monitoring clinic

    For parents of children with a home monitor: questions about monitoring, theory and practice of infant resuscitation.
